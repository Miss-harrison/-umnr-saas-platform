# UMNR Risks & Dependencies

## Overview

This document identifies key project risks and dependencies that could affect the successful delivery of the UMNR platform.

Risks should be reviewed throughout the project lifecycle and updated as development progresses.

---

## Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-001 | Incorrect role permissions expose restricted information | High | Medium | Validate RBAC rules and test access for each user role before release |
| R-002 | Inaccurate talent availability results in booking conflicts | High | Medium | Validate availability data and perform conflict checks before booking confirmation |
| R-003 | Scheduling changes create conflicting bookings | High | Medium | Trigger availability checks whenever booking dates are changed |
| R-004 | Growth in platform activity affects performance | Medium | Medium | Monitor performance and validate scalability during development and testing |
| R-005 | Sensitive documents are accessed by unauthorized users | High | Low | Apply access controls and validate document permissions |
| R-006 | Delays in foundational modules affect dependent features | High | Medium | Prioritize authentication, access control, and core talent functionality before dependent modules |
| R-007 | Incomplete requirements cause development rework | Medium | Medium | Review requirements with relevant stakeholders before implementation |

---

## Key Dependencies

### Authentication & Access Control

Authentication and RBAC are foundational dependencies for most platform functionality.

Dependent modules include:

- Talent management
- Casting
- Booking
- Messaging
- Documents
- Analytics
- Administration

---

### Talent Data

Accurate talent profiles and availability information are required before casting, booking, and scheduling workflows can operate reliably.

Dependent modules include:

- Talent discovery
- Casting shortlists
- Booking
- Calendar and scheduling

---

### Casting

Casting functionality depends on talent-management capabilities and user permissions.

Booking workflows may also use information generated during the casting process.

---

### Booking & Scheduling

Booking and scheduling functionality depends on:

- Talent availability
- User permissions
- Talent profiles
- Casting information

Accurate scheduling information is particularly important for preventing booking conflicts.

---

### Operational Data

Analytics and reporting depend on sufficient operational data being generated by the platform.

Relevant data sources include:

- Talent activity
- Castings
- Bookings
- Scheduling
- Financial activity

---

## Risk Monitoring Approach

Project risks should be reviewed regularly throughout delivery.

For each active risk, the project team should track:

- Risk owner
- Current status
- Mitigation action
- Target resolution date
- Impact on dependent milestones

Risks that threaten milestone completion should be escalated and reflected in project status reporting.
